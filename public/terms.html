Orders

An order is confirmed when the store accepts it. We may call you to confirm items, quantities or substitutions. If an item is unavailable, we will inform you and adjust the bill.

All prices are in Indian Rupees and include applicable taxes. Prices of fruits, vegetables and dairy may change daily. The final amount is the one shown on your order confirmation.

Delivery and pickup

Home delivery is available only in the pincodes accepted at checkout. A delivery charge applies below the free-delivery amount shown at checkout. Delivery times are estimates. Orders placed after closing time are processed the next morning. For store pickup, please bring your order number.

Payment

You can pay by UPI, or cash/UPI on delivery or at the store. UPI orders are processed after the store verifies the payment.

Coupons and points have their own conditions (minimum order, expiry, usage limit) and cannot be exchanged for cash. The store may change or end offers at any time.
