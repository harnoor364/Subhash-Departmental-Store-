What we collect

When you place an order or send an enquiry, we collect your name, mobile number, delivery address, landmark, pincode, order details and any notes you add. If you pay by UPI, we store the UPI reference (UTR) number you give us so we can match your payment.

How we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and track your order
  • To contact you about your order by phone, SMS or WhatsApp
  • To show your order history and loyalty points when you enter your mobile number
  • To improve our store and website

What we don't do

We do not sell your information. We share it only when needed to complete your order (for example, with a delivery person) or when the law requires it.

Payments

UPI payments go directly from your UPI app to the store's account. We never see or store your UPI PIN, card or bank details. If online card payment is offered, it is processed by a secure payment provider.

Cookies and analytics

Your cart and light/dark mode choice are saved in your own browser. We may use Google Analytics to understand how the website is used.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, contact us using the details on our contact page.
