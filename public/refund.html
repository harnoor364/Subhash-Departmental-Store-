Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order before it is packed by calling or messaging the store with your order number. Once an order is out for delivery or ready for pickup, it may not be possible to cancel.

Returns at delivery

Please check your items when you receive them. If something is damaged, expired, wrong or missing, tell the delivery person or contact us within [24 hours] and we will replace it or refund it.

Items we can't take back

For hygiene reasons, opened food items, fruits and vegetables (after acceptance), dairy and personal care products cannot be returned unless they were damaged or wrong.

Refunds

Approved refunds for UPI or online payments are sent back to the same UPI ID / account within [5–7 working days]. Cash payments are refunded in cash or as store credit.

Contact

For any issue, visit our contact page and share your order number.
